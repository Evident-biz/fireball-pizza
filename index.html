About

Flavor that leaves a mark.

At Fireball Pizza, we're all about flavor that leaves a mark. From our hand-crafted pizzas to our fully loaded calzones, every bite is built for maximum satisfaction. Our panuozzos and pizza rolls offer a bold twist on tradition, blending classic Italian inspiration with our own fiery flair.

Whether you're planning a private event or just craving something fresh and hot, Fireball Pizza brings the heat. If you're in Modesto, catch us at the Farmers Market every Thursday and Saturday.
