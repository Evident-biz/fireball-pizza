FAQ

Common Questions

How far in advance should I book?

There's no strict minimum, but the earlier you lock in your date, the better your odds of availability. Weddings are typically booked 6–12 months out; corporate events sometimes just a week or two in advance.

What guest count do you serve?

We comfortably handle events from 20 to 250+ guests.

How long do you need for setup?

We arrive early to set up and preheat the ovens, so everything's hot and ready when your guests are.

Do you need power or water at the venue?

No, we're fully self-sufficient. No power hookups or water access required at your site.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes, we offer gluten-free and vegetarian options so every guest is covered.

What areas do you serve?

Stanislaus County is our primary service area. We also regularly serve Tuolumne, Merced, San Joaquin, and Alameda Counties.

How does pricing work?

Buffet style is $19/person (choice of up to 5 pizzas, up to 250 guests). Build Your Own is $25/person (each guest builds their own, up to 40 guests). Pizza Workshop is $25/person (up to 40 kids), with an optional adult buffet add-on at $19/person for up to 100 additional guests. All three include salad and breadsticks. A 50% deposit is due at booking to lock in your date. Travel and labor fees may apply based on distance. Use the instant quote calculator above for an exact number.

What is Pizza Workshop?

Pizza Workshop is our hands-on option for kids' birthday parties. Our staff guides each kid through topping their own pizza before we cook it fresh on-site. It's $25/person for up to 40 kids, and you can add an adult buffet for up to 100 additional guests at $19/person.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept online payment, card, check, or cash.
