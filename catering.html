About Fireball Pizza

Live-Fire Catering, No Kitchen Required

We bring a real brick oven straight to your event — hand-stretched dough, fresh ingredients, and a crew that handles setup to cleanup. Whether it's an intimate backyard gathering or a 250-guest wedding, we show up ready to cook, right in front of your guests.

  • Brick oven, delivered and set up on-site
  • Fresh, hand-stretched dough made in-house
  • Crew sized to your event — 2 to 4 staff based on guest count
  • We arrive ~2 hours early to set up and preheat, so we're ready when your guests are
  • No power or water hookups needed — we're fully self-sufficient
  • Gluten-free, vegan, and vegetarian options available
  • Serving 20 to 250+ guests

FAQ

Common Questions

How far in advance should I book?

There's no strict minimum, but the earlier you lock in your date, the better your odds of availability. Weddings are typically booked 6–12 months out; corporate events sometimes just a week or two in advance.

What guest count do you serve?

We comfortably handle events from 20 to 250+ guests.

How long do you need for setup?

We arrive about 2 hours before your event start time to set up and preheat the ovens, so everything's hot and ready when your guests are.

Do you need power or water at the venue?

No — we're fully self-sufficient. No power hookups or water access required at your site.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes — we offer gluten-free, vegan, and vegetarian options so every guest is covered.

What areas do you serve?

Stanislaus County is our primary service area. We also regularly serve Tuolumne, Merced, and San Joaquin Counties.

How does pricing work?

Buffet style is $19/person (choice of up to 5 pizzas, up to 250 guests). Build Your Own is $25/person (each guest builds their own, up to 40 guests). Both include salad and breadsticks. A 50% deposit is due at booking to lock in your date. Travel and labor fees may apply based on distance — use the instant quote calculator above for an exact number.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept online payment, card, check, or cash.
